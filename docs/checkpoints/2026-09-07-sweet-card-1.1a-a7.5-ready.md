## Workflow

- Electronic Card Detail now has a mobile-first `电子卡发放` section. It edits the existing recipient, company, gifting-scenario, and recipient-note presentation fields; shows the batch business name and template version; generates/downloads the electronic card; explicitly activates it for delivery; and refreshes Claim and Binding facts from the server.
- The UI separately displays Card, activation, presentation, Claim credential, Claim, Binding, and delivery-preparation states. Raw enum values are mapped through the shared Sweet Card label helper. Card and Batch internal IDs are limited to the collapsed technical section.
- Batch amount input is `面额（元）`. The server converts a strict decimal string to integer cents with `BigInt`; storage and all economic authorities remain cents. Electronic immediate activation remains OFF by default.
- `生成电子卡` uses the accepted Test-only Claim credential service. The card QR is labelled `微信扫码领取甜意卡`; the POS credential generator and manufacturer export contract remain unchanged. Reissue requires an explicit second confirmation, revokes the former active Claim credential through the A7 service, and creates no economic fact.
- `激活并准备发放` reuses the existing activation transition and requires an electronic carrier plus a current active Claim credential. It does not introduce another lifecycle or economic status.
- Claim and Binding status come from current `SweetCardClaimToken`, `SweetCardClaim`, and `SweetCardBinding` rows on every detail refresh. Claimed cards expose only the claim time and safe business presentation; no OpenID or internal customer identifier is shown.

## Authority and safety
